Studio

Monk High Games

Monk High Games is the independent game studio through which Logan LaBarge develops his own games. Its current project is Amator Fortuna.

In development · PC (Windows)

Amator Fortuna

Amator Fortuna is a story-driven voxel survival game built in Unreal Engine 5. Players gather, craft, build and fight across a generated world of towns, factions and outlaw camps, where townsfolk keep their own jobs and daily routines and can hold conversations with the player. The game is in closed beta testing.

  • Built in Unreal Engine 5 (C++)
  • Crafting with metal tool tiers, from copper and bronze to iron and steel
  • Swords, bows, early firearms and cannons
  • Towns whose residents work, trade and react to the player
  • Co-op multiplayer in development
