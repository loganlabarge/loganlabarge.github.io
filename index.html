About

Logan LaBarge is a finance graduate and game developer based in Utah.

He earned his finance degree at Utah Valley University and is a Level II Candidate in the CFA Program. He has passed the Series 65 and Securities Industry Essentials (SIE) exams and holds a Microsoft Excel certification.

Logan is the founder of Monk High Games and the game and creative director of Amator Fortuna, the first-ever voxel RPG first-person shooter with integrated AI.

Amator Fortuna

Amator Fortuna is the first-ever voxel RPG first-person shooter with integrated AI, built in Unreal Engine 5. Logan is its game and creative director. Players gather, craft, build and fight across a generated world of towns, factions and outlaw camps, where townsfolk keep their own jobs and daily routines and can hold conversations with the player. The game is in closed beta testing.

  • Built in Unreal Engine 5 (C++)
  • Crafting with metal tool tiers, from copper and bronze to iron and steel
  • Swords, bows, early firearms and cannons
  • Towns whose residents work, trade and react to the player
  • Co-op multiplayer in development
