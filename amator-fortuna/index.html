About the game

Amator Fortuna is the first-ever voxel RPG first-person shooter with integrated AI, built in Unreal Engine 5.

Players gather, craft, build and fight across a generated world of towns, factions and outlaw camps, where townsfolk keep their own jobs and daily routines and can hold conversations with the player.

The game is developed by Monk High Games and is in closed beta testing.

Features

  • Integrated AI

    Townsfolk who talk back

    Residents keep their own jobs and daily routines, and can hold conversations with the player.

  • Living world

    Towns, factions and outlaw camps

    A generated world whose towns work, trade and react to what the player does.

  • Crafting

    Gather, craft and build

    Metal tool tiers, from copper and bronze to iron and steel.

  • Combat

    Blades to black powder

    Swords, bows, early firearms and cannons, played in first person.

  • Multiplayer

    Co-op

    Co-op multiplayer is in development.

  • Technology

    Unreal Engine 5

    Built in Unreal Engine 5 with C++.
